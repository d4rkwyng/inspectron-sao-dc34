INSPECTRON 34 — quick-turn assembly notes (paste into the order "special instructions" box)
=============================================================================================

QTY: 5 boards, full turnkey (you fabricate the bare board + source parts + assemble).

BOARD (fab):
- 2-layer FR-4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper.
- Surface finish: ENIG (required — needed for the hand-soldered display flex and the
  gold look). Please do NOT substitute HASL.
- Solder mask: matte black preferred. IF matte black adds lead time, green is an
  acceptable fallback for these units (ENIG is the hard requirement, mask color is not).
- Silkscreen: white.
- Min track/space 0.15/0.12 mm, min hole 0.2 mm (all within standard capability).

SOLDER MASK ON U1 (RP2040, QFN-56, 0.4 mm pitch):
- The pad-to-pad gap is 0.20 mm, so a solder-mask dam between those pins is below
  matte-black minimum. Please GANG / merge the mask into one opening over the QFN pad
  rows (web-out the sub-spec slivers). This is expected for a 0.4 mm QFN — do NOT enlarge
  the pads or alter the land pattern. Stencil/paste volume controls bridging.

ASSEMBLY:
- Double-sided SMT (parts on both top and bottom — see centroid "Side" column).
- DO NOT populate J3 (the TFT display). It is intentionally absent from the BOM and
  centroid; the customer hand-solders the display flex after delivery. Leave the J3 pads
  bare and clean.
- J1 is a THROUGH-HOLE 2x3 header: mount on the BACK side, solder from the front. (It is
  the only THT part.)
- Do not substitute any part carrying an MPN in the BOM (U1/U2/U3/Q1/Q2/D1/J2/LEDs).
  R/C passives may be substituted with equivalents (value + size + dielectric as noted).
- U3 must be the 3.3 V output LDO (ME6211C33). Q2 must be logic-level (fully on at
  Vgs <= 3 V). Y1 crystal ESR <= 100 ohm.

SHIPPING: fastest domestic (overnight if available). Hard deadline: in hand before Aug 6.
